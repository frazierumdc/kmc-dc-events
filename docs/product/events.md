# F1 — Events

> Requirements for the first feature.
> IDs (`EV-*`, `NFR-*`) are stable: cite them in commits and pull requests.
> Pictures are mock-ups made from the archived prototype ([workflow/mockups.md](../workflow/mockups.md)).
> They set the app's look (D45); the notes and buttons they show that the website doesn't have are left out (D36), and this text gives the exact rules.
> All of them: [gallery](../design/README.md#f1-events-list).
> Built in slices 1.1 – 1.4 of [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md).

## Where events come from

The app shows exactly what the center's website shows (D36).
It adds nothing of its own and corrects nothing: if something should show in the app, it belongs on the website, in Wix.

```
Staff create or change an event in Wix (as today)
      │
      ▼  at 4 AM and 4 PM Eastern, or Sync now
The update job reads the website's events through one adapter
      │
      ▼  only when something changed
A new schedule is published; management staff get a "Schedule changes" email (F2)
      │
      ▼
Phones pick up the new schedule next time they open
```

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| EV-1 | **Published events only.** The app lists only events published on the center's Wix site. Drafts, cancelled and ended events never appear (D3, D8). |
| EV-2 | **Today onward.** The app holds events from the start of today (Eastern) to 400 days ahead. |
| EV-3 | **As the website shows them.** Each event shows what the website shows: title, date and time, location, description, the website's category names, and whether registration is open. Events the website lists twice are listed the same way (D36); an online event is listed with its in-person event (EV-40). |
| EV-4 | **No details of the app's own.** The app adds no notes, labels, buttons or corrections that aren't on the website, and no event is hidden or changed by hand. The exceptions are an event's kind, which follows from its website category (D45), and the online schedule copied from the website's online classes page (D46). |
| EV-5 | **One adapter.** Only the adapter knows the source's format (D37). It turns each event into a neutral *website event*; an event it can't read (no title or start) is left out and listed in the job's summary. |
| EV-6 | **Real links only.** Register and More info open the event's own page on example.org (an allowed host), or for another center's event its page on that center's website (EV-43). Links are never guessed. Web addresses in a description are shown as tappable links, as on the website. |
| EV-7 | **Ids from the source.** An event's id is the source's own id, so its link stays the same for as long as the event exists in Wix (D39). |
| EV-8 | **Copied, not reviewed.** The update job runs twice a day, at 4 AM and 4 PM Eastern, and on demand (Sync now), and publishes a new schedule only when something changed (D38). |
| EV-9 | **Safe when the source misbehaves.** If the source can't be read, its data is malformed, or it has no events while the published schedule has 20 or more, nothing is published, the app keeps showing the last published schedule, and admins are alerted (F2). |

Each event has a **kind** (its type) from its website category, through one fixed table in the site settings, and the kind's own colour (D45).
Maroon is never a kind's colour: it marks only the app's own highlights.

| Kind | Colour | Website categories |
|---|---|---|
| **Classes** | emerald | Weekly Meditation Classes, Study Programs, Richmond Branch Classes |
| **Special** | red-orange | Retreats & Special Events, Online Special Events |
| **Prayers** | lapis | Chanted Prayers |

Two groups of classes have a colour of their own (D45): a study program's session, the Foundation Program and the Teacher Training Program alike, **amethyst**, told by its category and title as its emails tell it (AN-15); and a branch class, in a category named "… Branch Classes" (today Richmond Branch Classes), **sapphire**.
Its block, bar and the details panel's category pill take that colour; the filter and the dots under the dates keep Classes' emerald.
These are light mode's colours; dark mode uses teal Classes, orange Special, violet Prayers, blue FP and TTP and rose branch classes (owner, 9 November 2026).
An event with several categories takes the first kind in the order Special, Classes, Prayers.
A category not in the table gives no kind: its events show under All, without colour, and the update job's summary names the category so it can be added.

### Online option

An event at the center can also be attended online (D46).
There are no online-only events.

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| EV-40 | **Online option.** An event has one of three online options, or none. **Livestream:** a weekly class whose Eastern weekday and start time are on the website's online classes page, or a chanted prayer other than Dakini Yoga and Quick Path to Great Bliss; **Join online** opens the online classes page, where members log in for the Zoom link. The schedule is copied into `online.livestreams` in `data/site.json`. **Online event:** a retreat or special event whose separate online event in Wix starts at the same time (and shares the most title words); it isn't listed on its own, and **Register online** (or **More info**) opens its page. **Online icon only:** a festival or post-festival retreat ("Retreats & Special Events" with the word "Festival" in its title), wherever it is held, shows the online icon in the list; its details panel has no online button and Register stays Register. The rule is `online.iconOnly` in `data/site.json`. Every other event (study programs, Richmond, other events away from the center) is in person only. No Zoom link, meeting id or passcode is ever in the app. |

The place is **KMC DC** (the center's short name) for the center's city, otherwise the city as Wix has it ("Richmond"), or the venue's name when Wix gives no city; an online event shows only Online. The place stands out: a filled pin and the place in the title's colour, semi-bold; Online stays in the second line's grey with a line icon. No chips and no colour of their own: icon, word and weight tell the two apart, never colour, which keeps meaning the kind (D45). **Every card is the same height:** each keeps the room of two title lines, the where line and the second line, even when one is empty, so a special event with a long name is no taller than a class. At very large text sizes cards grow to fit and nothing is cut: the time sits beside the picture and the title and its lines have the whole width below. The picture is decoration: the kind's colour fills its square while it loads; when the event has none, or it can't load with no connection, the center's logo shows on that colour (owner, 16 November 2026); a picture once shown is kept for use with no connection, and only a picture: a missing one is never kept in its place. Screen readers hear the row's name, ending with where it is ("at KMC DC, also online", "in Richmond", "Online"). Cards are rounder on iPhone (18 points) than on Android (16 dp). A row doesn't darken, ripple or light up when pressed or after its panel closes: nothing in the app flashes. |
| EV-12 | **Kind filter:** All · Classes · Special · Prayers, always these four, each kind with a dot of its colour, so the filter is also the colour key. On iPhone it is a capsule segmented control, each segment 44 pt tall; on Android, Material's connected buttons, one outlined capsule split by lines, each button 48 dp tall, the selected one tinted maroon (option A, owner, 13 November 2026). The selected name is semibold on both. Every name shows whole, on a 360-point screen at the largest text too. One is always selected; the list and the date dots follow it. A special event the website also lists as a class is under Classes as well as Special, in its Special colour (D45). **Swiping the list sideways** changes it, as tabs do on iPhone and Android: a finger moving right to left goes to the next filter (All → Classes → Special → Prayers), left to right to the previous one, stopping at All and Prayers, where the list stretches a little and springs back. The list follows the finger a little, and the filter's selection with it: on iPhone the segmented control's thumb (white; in dark, a grey clearly lighter than the control) slides along with the finger (and slides to a tapped segment); on Android the button letting go would choose is tinted as soon as it would be chosen. Let go far or fast enough and the list leaves at the finger's speed and the next filter's list comes in from the other side on a spring; otherwise it springs back, keeping the finger's speed. The next list comes in only once it has been drawn, and the top bar, filter and row of dates stay on screen throughout, holding still, at their size, and blurring the list under them as it comes in; the marked date holds still during the swipe. With Reduce Motion nothing slides. **Tapping a filter** selects it at once and slides the list the same way, toward the later or earlier filter, one short slide however many it jumps. Up-and-down scrolling, taps, the row of dates and swipes from a screen edge (the phone's own gestures) are left alone. |
| EV-13 | **Row of dates.** A sideways-swipeable row from today to the last event: weekday, date number, one dot for each kind among the day's matching events, in the order Classes, Special, Prayers (a day of five prayers and a class shows an emerald and a lapis dot; a grey dot only when the day's events have no kind), and the month and year above the first day and each 1st ("Oct 2026"); the month in view stays whole at the row's start as it scrolls, until the next month's name pushes it out; screen readers hear each date with its year. The month names and today's number are maroon; the current date (the day at the top of the list) is filled maroon, with white dots (D45). |
| EV-14 | **The dates follow the list.** The current date is the first listed day with anything showing below the filter and the row of dates, so it is always the first date the reader sees (at the top of the list, the first listed day: today when it has events left). Scrolling the list moves it, and the row scrolls sideways to keep it in view. The list ends with just enough room for its last day to reach the top. Tapping a date moves the list, in one step, so that day starts just under the row, and focus goes to its heading, which is not highlighted; a date with nothing listed (no events, or none matching the filter) moves to the next listed day, or the last when none follows. **Changing the filter keeps the date:** the current date, or the next day the new filter lists (the last when none follows), starts just under the row and is current, its maroon fill sliding over from the date marked before when both are in view (not with Reduce Motion); at the top of the list it stays at the top. A date moved to stays current until the reader scrolls, by touch or by tapping the status bar at the top of an iPhone, which scrolls to the top and marks the first date. The list always shows every day that matches the filter. |
| EV-15 | **Remembered.** The kind filter is remembered between launches; the place in the list is not. A remembered choice the app no longer offers falls back to All. |
| EV-16 | **Empty message:** "No events match this filter" when nothing is left to list. |
| EV-17 | **Footer.** A membership card. The card is neutral (no kind colour): the heading **You belong here**, "Become a member and make meditation part of your life.", three benefits with grey check marks (free drop-in classes, free Friday Night Lectures, free or half-price courses and retreats), an outlined maroon **See membership plans** button that opens the website's pricing plans page, and "Benefits vary by plan." in small grey. Screen readers hear the card as a region named by its heading, and the benefits as a list. Nothing is ever hidden at the bottom of the screen. |

On opening and after 5 minutes or more away the splash covers this (EV-42), so the list the reader first sees is already the newest. |

The two things an update may say are in that panel (NT-5): "Updated just now" when its date, time or place changed, and "This event is no longer on the schedule" when its event was removed. |
| EV-21 | **Offline and self-updating.** After the first visit the app opens and works with no connection. New versions download in the background and apply when the app is opened or returned to (at once if the download finishes within a few seconds of that, otherwise on the next return; never mid-read), with nothing to press and nothing said. A newer schedule says nothing either (EV-19). Nobody updates the app by hand. |
| EV-22 | **Feels native on both phones.** iPhone: system font, a frosted top bar, 58 points tall with room around the 36-point logo, the title and the filter, that always shows the logo and "Events" in small with "Kadampa Meditation Center DC" under it in grey, as a navigation bar's title and subtitle, centred, with the logo at the start and **Settings** (a gear, maroon) at the end (EV-41), and no large title, so the list keeps most of the screen; the filter as a joined segmented control, each event its own rounded block. There is no tab bar: the list runs to the bottom, and messages float above the home bar. Light and dark follow the phone. Fits any screen, avoiding the notch and home bar. |

## Qualities

| ID | Quality | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| NFR-1 | Fast | The splash shows from the first frame; the list is ready behind it within 1 second after the first visit, online or offline, and within 3 seconds on a first visit on 4G, so the splash (EV-42) ends on time. Initial JavaScript ≤ 150 KB gzip. A tap on a row starts the panel's slide at once: on a mid-range phone no pause over 50 ms and no dropped frames while it opens or closes. |
| NFR-2 | Accessible | WCAG 2.2 AA. Every date read as "Monday, October 5, 3 events"; selected filters, dates and tabs announced as selected; Reduce Motion respected. Text follows the phone's text size (iPhone Text Size and Larger Text, Android font size): the list grows fully, the filter and the row of dates at most 1.3×, tab labels stay fixed; at very large sizes the time moves above the title. Nothing needs sideways scrolling, and nothing is hidden behind the bars. |
| NFR-3 | Accurate | Times correct in Eastern time across daylight-saving changes; registration links real (EV-6). |
| NFR-4 | Dependable | If Wix, GitHub or the network is down, the app still opens with the last schedule. |
| NFR-5 | Private | No accounts, tracking or third-party requests. The privacy page (IN-9), in Info and at `/privacy`, says "KMC DC Events collects no data about you". |
| NFR-6 | Cheap | $0 a month at launch (free tiers of Firebase Hosting and GitHub Actions). |
| NFR-7 | Secure | Secrets (feed token, Gmail credentials) never in code, logs, pull requests or the app. |
| NFR-8 | Browsers | Current Safari, Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Samsung Internet. |
